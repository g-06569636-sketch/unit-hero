⬇️ LARGER UNIT → SMALLER UNIT

Multiply by conversion factor:

  • • 1 km = 1,000 m (× 1,000)
  • • 1 m = 100 cm (× 100)
  • • 1 cm = 10 mm (× 10)
  • • 1 kg = 1,000 g (× 1,000)
  • • 1 L = 1,000 mL (× 1,000)

⬆️ SMALLER UNIT → LARGER UNIT

Divide by conversion factor:

  • • 1,000 m = 1 km (÷ 1,000)
  • • 100 cm = 1 m (÷ 100)
  • • 10 mm = 1 cm (÷ 10)
  • • 1,000 g = 1 kg (÷ 1,000)
  • • 1,000 mL = 1 L (÷ 1,000)
